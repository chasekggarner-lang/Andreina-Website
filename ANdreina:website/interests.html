Interests

01

My Journey

My journey at Rutgers actually started very differently. I entered college as a Biological Sciences major with plans to pursue a career in medicine. As I continued through school, I realized that path wasn’t the right fit for me and made the decision to switch to Human Resource Management. I was drawn to HR because of its focus on people, communication, workplace culture, and creating better experiences within organizations.

Eventually, I also switched my minor from Psychology to DCIM after realizing how much I enjoyed digital communication and media. Through my classes and involvement in the Rutgers community, I discovered an interest in graphic design, social media, content creation, and digital storytelling.

A major part of that discovery came from taking on marketing and leadership positions within student organizations. I’ve had the opportunity to help organizations strengthen their online presence, create content, promote events, and develop a more consistent public image. Seeing my work help an organization grow and connect with more students made me realize how much I enjoy using creativity with a purpose.

02

What I Do

Whether I’m creating content, developing a marketing strategy, collaborating with a team, planning an event, or working directly with customers, I enjoy understanding people and finding better ways to communicate and connect with them.

Through my coursework, professional experience, and leadership involvement, I’ve developed skills in content creation, Canva, social media strategy, digital communication, Microsoft Office, outreach, organization, Adobe, teamwork, leadership, and customer relations.

03

Beyond My Work

Outside of school and work, culture and community are still a big part of who I am. I love staying connected to my Dominican culture, spending time with the people I care about, and being part of spaces that allow me to celebrate where I come from and connect with others.

Creativity is also something I enjoy beyond a screen. Even though a lot of my work now involves digital design and media, I still love creating things with my hands. Whether I’m making artwork, putting together craft pieces, decorating, or simply starting a new creative project for fun, I enjoy having an idea in my head and watching it turn into something I can actually see and hold.

04

Looking Into the Future

As I prepare to graduate, I’m excited to continue exploring opportunities that allow me to combine my background in Human Resources with my interests in digital marketing, communications, and media. I’m especially interested in roles where I can continue learning, creating, connecting with people, and making an impact.

My career path may not look exactly like what I imagined when I first started college, but that’s what makes it mine. Every change along the way has helped me better understand what I enjoy, what I’m good at, and the kind of professional I want to become.
